The Story

Many Lives.
One Direction.

Born in New York. Raised on hard work and harder lessons. Spent years moving — South Carolina, Virginia, Florida, Ohio, Texas, California — wearing every hat imaginable. Kitchens, door-to-door sales, every kind of grind there is.

Then came the international chapter. Istanbul. Dubai. Alexandria. Cairo. Somewhere along the way, Egypt stopped being a destination and became home. I married Ahd here, became stepdad to Maria, and then Serene arrived in 2026. That changed the stakes.

At 45, most people are winding down. I'm still learning, still building, and still trying to make the next chapter bigger than the last.
