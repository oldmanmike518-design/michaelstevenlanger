About

Sales first. Then I build.

I’m originally from New York and now live in Alexandria, Egypt, with my family.

Background

Most of my working life has been in sales. I have sold door to door and over the phone, and today I work in sales at Swivl, an AI robotics company that sells to schools.

The building started with that work. When I ran into a repeated task or a handoff that kept breaking, I wrote a script or a small tool for it. Some of those tools stayed small. Others turned into larger projects, including Prompt Duel and Agent Sandbox, which I built on my own time. You can read about them on the work page.

I also do bug bounty research on HackerOne. It makes me a more careful builder and tester.

After years of moving around the United States, I spent time in Egypt, Turkey, and the UAE. Egypt became home, and Alexandria is where I live now.
