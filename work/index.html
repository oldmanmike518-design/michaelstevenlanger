Live product

Prompt Duel

Groq · ElevenLabs · Firebase · Stripe · Vercel
What it is
A voice game where you speak your moves against AI opponents. You can play Arena duels, a five round Interrogation against an opponent named Graves, or a six round Tribunal. There are ten AI opponents across two worlds, and the judging looks at tactics and creativity, not word count.
The problem it addresses
A game built on spoken moves needs a fair judge. The scoring has to reward tactics instead of length, and it has to run on the server rather than in the player’s browser. It also has to handle accounts, payments, and voice usage for real players.
My role
I designed and built it on my own: the game design, the AI judging, progress from guest play to a full account, Stripe subscriptions, voice quotas, testing, and releases.
What you can check
The product is live and open to play. The latest release, in September 2026, was a story mode redesign with illustrated Interrogation and Tribunal showdowns, plus a security release for billing and voice quotas.

Live service, open source

Agent Sandbox

Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Redis
What it is
An interoperability check for autonomous AI agents. You point an agent at a neutral conformance partner over a public API, and you get back a permanent report you can cite, plus a badge for your README.
The problem it addresses
Agents can fail in ordinary ways when they meet software they were not built around. Agent Sandbox tests for a few of those: how an agent handles cursors, whether it avoids duplicate actions, and whether it treats hostile payloads as data instead of instructions.
My role
I built the service and wrote the checks.
What you can check
The service is public and the source code is open. It was relaunched in September 2026 as interoperability verification, with permanent public reports and badges.

Internal tools, not public

Swivl

CRM data · AI voice · Sales workflows
What it is
Swivl is an AI robotics company that sells to schools. I work in sales there, and I have built a set of tools, scripts, and workflows for the team.
The problem it addresses
Outbound sales has a lot of repeated work: researching districts, placing calls, booking meetings, and following up after discovery calls. I look for the parts that repeat and build tools for them.
My role
These are specific pieces I built and contributed to the team, alongside the rest of the sales process:
  • An AI calling workflow
  • Personalized one pagers for prospects
  • Automated meeting booking
  • A cadence and triage tool for follow up after discovery calls, with a flow canvas, opportunity triage, and a process editor in one app
  • A territory map built on CRM data
  • A district research tool that turns a school district into a sourced brief
  • A set of AI tools for sales development reps
What you can check
These are internal tools, so there is no public link. I do not publish screenshots, customer names, or customer data.
